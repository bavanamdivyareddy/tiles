About Us

Wanaparthy's Most Trusted Tiles Partner

Founded by B. Venkataram Reddy, Patel & Co has been the cornerstone of beautiful homes in Wanaparthy and the surrounding region for over 15 years. What started as a small showroom has grown into one of the most trusted names in premium tiles and sanitary ware in Telangana.

We believe your home deserves the best. That's why we curate only high-grade, certified products from leading manufacturers — combining Italian elegance, modern Indian design sensibilities, and unmatched durability.
